Drone Racer — free 3D FPV drone racing in your browser

Fly a first-person-view racing quadcopter through neon gate courses. Set time trials on replay-verified leaderboards, join online races and hourly knockout tournaments, battle in team PvP arenas, or design and publish your own tracks with the map editor.

How it works

Physics are fully deterministic, so the server replays every submitted run to confirm your time. Works with keyboard or gamepad; no download or install needed.
