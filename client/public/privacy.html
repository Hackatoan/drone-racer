Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Operator: Hackatoa · Contact: server@hackatoa.com

Drone Racer is a free browser game. This page explains what we store and why.

Playing as a guest

You can fly, free roam and join online rooms without an account. Your settings, keybinds, map drafts and personal-best run files are stored only in your browser (localStorage). Online play sends your drone's position and your temporary guest name to the game server while you are connected; it is not stored.

Signing in

Sign-in uses Google through Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). We receive a Firebase user id and verify your sign-in token; we do not receive your Google password. We store: your chosen pilot name, leaderboard runs (the input recording of each run, its time and date), maps, textures and reviews you publish, reports you file, friend connections, direct messages with friends, and game statistics (races, wins, kills, tournament points).

Run verification

To keep leaderboards fair, while you fly a time trial your game sends timing heartbeats to the server, and each uploaded run is re-simulated. These checks use only game data.

Payments

Premium is sold through Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, its amount, and the session id, linked to your account.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Firebase may store sign-in state in your browser. See the cookie policy.

Public content

Your pilot name, published maps, reviews, leaderboard times and run recordings are visible to everyone and may be downloaded as replays.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. Email server@hackatoa.com to access or delete your data. Moderators may hide or remove reported content.

Children

Drone Racer is intended for players 13 and older. Players under 18 need a parent's permission to make a purchase.

See also the general Hackatoa privacy policy.
